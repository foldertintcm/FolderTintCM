## English (en-US)

**Name**: FolderTintCM

**Short description**:

> Colours for your folders, as on a Mac. Right-click a folder → FolderTintCM
> → pick one of 13 colours or create your own. Find your projects at a
> glance.

**Description**:

> FolderTintCM colours your Windows 10 and 11 folders with a right-click.
> No window to open and nothing else to change about how you work: the
> folders that matter stand out at once.
>
> ONE RIGHT-CLICK
> • Right-click a folder → FolderTintCM → pick a colour.
> • On Windows 11 it is in the first menu, without "Show more options".
> • Also on the background of an open folder, and on several selected
>   folders at once.
> • "Remove colour" puts it back as it was.
>
> THIRTEEN COLOURS, PLUS YOURS
> • Apple's 13 colours: red, orange, yellow, green, mint, teal, cyan, blue,
>   indigo, purple, pink, brown and gray.
> • "Custom…" opens a picker with a live preview of the folder, a colour
>   square, a hue bar and a HEX code.
> • Your last 6 colours show up in the menu, one click away.
> • Icons drawn to stay sharp in every view, from details to extra large
>   icons.
>
> IT STAYS
> • The colour is kept in the folder itself: it survives restarts and
>   Windows updates, and travels with the folder when you move or rename it.
> • Uninstalling gives every folder its usual icon back.
>
> LIGHT AND PRIVATE
> • Nothing runs in the background: each menu entry does its job and closes
>   in an instant.
> • No internet connection: no accounts, no telemetry, no ads.
> • Never touches system folders or icons you chose by hand.
> • In Spanish and English, in light or dark mode.
> • Open source (GPL-3.0).

**Product features**:

1. Colour folders from File Explorer's right-click menu
2. On Windows 11, in the first menu, without "Show more options"
3. Apple's 13 colours
4. Its own colour picker with a live preview and HEX codes
5. Your last 6 colours, in the menu
6. Several folders at once
7. "Remove colour" puts the folder back as it was
8. The colour stays after restarts and when you move the folder
9. No background processes, no internet connection
10. Light and dark mode, in Spanish and English
